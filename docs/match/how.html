Cofounder matching

How pairs are decided

Two steps: every possible pair gets a score from both people’s rankings, then pairs are formed starting from the best score.

1. How a pair is scored

You rank the people you would want to build with, best fit first. For any two people, take where each ranked the other, square both numbers and add them. Lower is better, and both sides count equally.

You ranked themThey ranked youScore
1st1st1 + 1 = 2
2nd4th4 + 16 = 20

If someone is not on your list at all, that counts as worse than any ranked position.

2. How pairs are formed

  1. Look at every pair where both people ranked each other.
  2. Pair up the two with the best score. They are now matched and leave the pool.
  3. Repeat with everyone who is left, until no such pairs remain.

Your score with another person depends only on how the two of you ranked each other. How anyone else ranked anyone else never changes it. Other people’s choices matter in just one way: someone you ranked may be matched first with a person they score better with.

Anyone left at the end, with nobody remaining who ranked them back, is paired with the best available fit.

What this means for you

  • Rank honestly, in your real order of preference.
  • Rank everyone you would be glad to build with. A longer list gives you more chances at a mutual match, and anyone on it is someone you could end up paired with.
  • Your ranking is seen only by the organizers, who review the final pairings.
